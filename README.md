PNLP_H1N8_Pathogens

Supporting data and analysis files for:

Genomic Characterization of a Reassortant A/H1N8 Avian Influenza Virus in South American Migratory Shorebirds Fenner G.O., Thomazelli L.M., Dorlass E.G., Eisen A.K.A., Reis N.A., Durigon Junior E.L., Carvalho T.P., Monezi T.A., Ozaki D.Y.S.R., Silva A.L.C., Brum A.C., Haas M.L., Finger J.V.G., Petry M.V., Feigin S., Niles L., Barboza M.G.L., Vizu F.P., Kraiser T., Durigon E.L., de Araujo J. Manuscript prepared for submission to Pathogens (MDPI).

Summary

This study reports the detection and evolutionary characterization of a reassortant low pathogenic avian influenza virus (LPAI) subtype H1N8, isolated from a White-rumped Sandpiper (Calidris fuscicollis) sampled at Parque Nacional da Lagoa do Peixe (PNLP), southern Brazil. Genomic analyses showed that five internal gene segments (PB2, PB1, PA, NP, MP) share a North American-derived genomic backbone with a previously described PNLP H11N2 virus, while the HA, NA, and NS segments were acquired from locally circulating South American lineages. Findings are supported by maximum-likelihood phylogenetics (IQ-TREE2), Bayesian phylogenetic/phylodynamic reconstruction (BEAST X), discrete phylogeographic and host-state inference (BSSVS), and reassortment detection (TreeSort).

Repository contents
Relaxed_XMLs/ — BEAST XML input files run under the uncorrelated lognormal relaxed molecular clock.
Strict_XMLs/ — BEAST XML input files run under the strict molecular clock.
jackknife_XMLs/ — Jackknife replicate BEAST XMLs (5 replicates randomly removing 25% of Chilean/Argentinian sequences), used to assess sensitivity to sampling imbalance.
shared_backbone_XMLs/ — BEAST XMLs for the independent Bayesian analyses of the five shared internal gene segments (PB2, PB1, PA, NP, MP) linking the PNLP H11N2 and H1N8 viruses.
treesort_files/ — Input alignments/trees and annotated output from TreeSort reassortment inference.
MCC_trees.zip — Maximum clade credibility trees (TreeAnnotator output) for all Bayesian analyses.
ML_trees.zip — Maximum-likelihood phylogenetic trees (IQ-TREE2) for each of the 8 gene segments.
Sanger Ab1 - 222 Chromatograms.zip — Sanger sequencing chromatogram (.ab1) files for isolate PNLP_222.

Raw NGS sequencing reads (FASTQ) referenced in the manuscript are also hosted in this repository.
